claude> I wrote `ants.py`, a NumPy and Matplotlib simulation in which clear trails form between the nests and the food. The ants have no information about where the nest or food is. Each ant has three short sensors (left, front, right) that only smell pheromone, and it learns it has reached a nest or a food pile only when it steps onto one.

**How it works:** Ants looking for food leave "home" pheromone and follow "food" pheromone. Ants carrying food do the opposite. An ant leaves less scent the longer it has been since it last touched a nest or food, so each trail gets stronger toward its source. Pheromone slowly fades and spreads. There are 600 ants, 2 nests and 3 food piles of 3000 units each, run for 10,000 steps. About 15% of the ants are scouts that wander more.

**Problems found and fixed along the way:**
